Purpose
Compiled from plant samples submitted to the Plant Pathology, Entomology & Weed Science Department. Assists in identification and management of common agricultural problems.
Scope
Covers fungal, bacterial, viral, protozoal diseases; nematodes; physiological disorders; and insect pests affecting major crops including plantain, cassava, coconut, pineapple, tomato, and more.
